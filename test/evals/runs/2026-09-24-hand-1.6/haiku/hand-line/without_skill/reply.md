# Build Time Optimization Trend

Median build times have improved significantly across the last six releases, dropping from 14 minutes in v1 to just 6 minutes in v6:

```
Median Build Times (minutes)

15 |
14 | ●
13 |  \
12 |   ●---●
11 |     \
10 |      \
 9 |       ●
 8 |      / \
 7 |     /   \
 6 |    /     ●
 5 |
   +--+--+--+--+--+--
    v1 v2 v3 v4 v5 v6
```

This represents a 57% reduction in build time from v1 to v6, with the most significant improvements occurring in v4 (8 min, -33% from v3) and v6 (6 min, -33% from v5).
