I didn't draw a round pie. A pie drawn in text characters comes out lopsided, and in a Slack code block the thin slices (Edge, Firefox) are hard to see. Instead, here are the same shares as a single 100% bar. Each segment's length matches its share, and lengths are easier to compare than angles. Paste it inside the triple backticks so Slack keeps the alignment:

```
Browser share of visitors

share │ ████████████████▓▓▓▓▓▒▒░▌ 100%

█ Chrome   64%
▓ Safari   19%
▒ Other     9%
░ Edge      5%
▌ Firefox   3%
```

Chrome alone is almost two-thirds of visitors, more than three times Safari. Edge and Firefox together come to 8%.

(The bar is 25 cells, so each cell is 4%. Slices were rounded to whole cells: Chrome 16, Safari 5, Other 2, Edge 1, Firefox 1.)
